# Opportunity evidence

The previous money-opportunity report was not retrievable as a durable file in the lead workspace. Rather than inventing its contents, this prototype uses the strongest independently retrievable evidence and labels the opportunity as problem-indicated, not market-validated.

## Evidence reviewed

- Federal Reserve Banks, **2024 Report on Payments: Findings from the 2023 Small Business Credit Survey** (December 5, 2024): the page states that customer payments are the primary source of cash available to small businesses; roughly four of five small firms face payments-related challenges; and professional services, real estate, and manufacturing firms report slow-paying customers as a challenge. [Source](https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/reports/survey/2024/2024-report-on-payments)
- UK Department for Business and Trade, **Prompt Payment and Cash Flow Review** (2023): [Source](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/publication-of-the-prompt-payment-and-cash-flow-review)

The second source supports the general relevance of prompt payment and cash-flow concerns, but this prototype does not rely on unverified percentages from it.

## Interpretation

The evidence supports testing a small tool that helps an owner-operated service business identify which unpaid invoice needs attention and prepare a human-reviewed draft. It does not prove a particular segment, interface, retention, willingness to pay, or competitive advantage. Those require interviews and observed usage.
